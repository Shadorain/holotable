Optional small-model review · manual

Export the current scope, inspect the downloaded evidence, then choose a small model explicitly. Exporting and refreshing never call a model. The prompt contains aggregate measurements, coverage limits and evidence references—not transcripts or access tokens.

From the download directory, replace YOUR_SMALL_MODEL with your model selector:

omp --model YOUR_SMALL_MODEL --no-tools --no-extensions --no-skills --no-rules --no-title --no-session --max-time 2m -p @holotable-intel-review.txt

This produces advisory interpretation, not an independent code check or permission to change missions. Two minutes is a wall-time limit, not a price cap. Model charges belong to the separate OMP session; they are not automatically added to this operation’s spend.

Self-checks, reviews and landing are evidence of process—not externally verified correctness. Known cost is an estimate, not a bill.
